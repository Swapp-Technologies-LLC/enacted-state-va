## 2. Evaluation & Sourcing Determination
Prince William County adopts Zoning Text Amendments (ZTAs) through the Board of County Supervisors. The Planning Office publishes a list of "Recently Adopted Ordinances Not Yet Codified" linking individual plan numbers to the County's EnerGov Citizen Self Service platform (`egcss.pwcgov.org`). However, the official comprehensive Zoning Ordinance (Chapter 32) is published exclusively via **Municode**, a commercial aggregator on the hard blocklist.

Under Swapp Technologies LLC record integrity policy (**PROVENANCE.md**):
> "Sourcing: Primary government portals only. Hard blocklist: Municode, AmLegal (amlegal.com), eCode360, EnCodePlus."  
> "A jurisdiction that is empty because its code lives only on blocklisted aggregators is a **valid, recorded outcome** (`SOURCING_BLOCKED.md` noting what was checked) — never a gap to paper over."

Extracting adopted text directly from Municode is prohibited. Harvesting standalone adopted resolution PDFs from the dynamic EnerGov portal or Board meeting archives requires an authenticated/session-handling poller scheduled for post-launch expansion. In accordance with the Maryland-first release gate, Prince William County remains documented and unpopulated.
