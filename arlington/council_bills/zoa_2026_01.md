 provision of the public open spaces identified in the Plan, and the preservation of significant trees
 identified in the Plan or their replacement in accordance with the county's tree replacement guidelines.
 §9.3.1. Floor area ratio
 A site plan project in the "Revitalization Area" designated in the Plan may exceed 3.24 F.A.R.
 with bonus density for achieving certain goals consistent with the intent of the Plan, such as
 LEED, provided that development is consistent with the design guidelines, including building
 height maximums, and where it furthers the purposes of the Plan.
