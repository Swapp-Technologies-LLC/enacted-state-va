## Enacted text

```diff
Be it ordained that Article 16 of the Arlington County Zoning Ordinance is hereby amended, reenacted and recodified to enable expansions and additions to nonconforming two-family dwellings in Article 6 RA districts:

§16.2. Nonconforming Buildings and Structures
- 16.2.1. General
+ 16.2.2. Expansions and additions to nonconforming two-family dwellings in Article 6 RA districts shall be permitted subject to the setback and lot coverage standards of the underlying zoning district.
```
