## 2. Evaluation & Sourcing Determination
Loudoun County considers and adopts Zoning Ordinance Amendments (ZOAMs) through the Board of Supervisors (`loudoun.gov/bosdocuments`). However, the County Department of Planning and Zoning's primary official portal for "Adopted Zoning Ordinance Amendments" (`loudoun.gov/2935/Zoning-Ordinance-Amendments-ZOAMS`) explicitly delegates hosting and publication of adopted amendment text to **EnCodePlus**, a commercial proprietary code platform. Furthermore, general county ordinances are hosted on **Municode**.

Under Swapp Technologies LLC record integrity policy (**PROVENANCE.md**):
> "Sourcing: Primary government portals only. Hard blocklist: Municode, AmLegal (amlegal.com), eCode360, EnCodePlus."  
> "A jurisdiction that is empty because its code lives only on blocklisted aggregators is a **valid, recorded outcome** (`SOURCING_BLOCKED.md` noting what was checked) — never a gap to paper over."

Direct ingestion from EnCodePlus or Municode is barred by the blocklist. Direct ingestion of standalone adopted amendment PDFs from the Board of Supervisors document repository requires a dedicated agenda attachment harvester scheduled for Phase 2. As of this evaluation, Loudoun County remains documented and unpopulated in accordance with the Maryland-first and DMV expansion release rules.
