## 2. Evaluation & Sourcing Determination
The City of Manassas adopts zoning and land-use ordinances through the Manassas City Council. The Council's legislative meetings, agenda packets, and action records are indexed via the City's official Legistar portal (`manassas.legistar.com`). However, the codified Code of the City of Manassas, including Chapter 130 (Zoning), is published and hosted solely on **Municode**, a commercial platform on the hard blocklist.

Under Swapp Technologies LLC record integrity policy (**PROVENANCE.md**):
> "Sourcing: Primary government portals only. Hard blocklist: Municode, AmLegal (amlegal.com), eCode360, EnCodePlus."  
> "A jurisdiction that is empty because its code lives only on blocklisted aggregators is a **valid, recorded outcome** (`SOURCING_BLOCKED.md` noting what was checked) — never a gap to paper over."

Ingesting zoning regulations from Municode is prohibited. Automated extraction of individual signed ordinances from the Legistar calendar is scheduled for Phase 2. The City of Manassas remains documented and unpopulated in compliance with the release rules.
