## 2. Evaluation & Sourcing Determination
The City of Fairfax adopts zoning amendments through the Fairfax City Council. The Council's legislative meetings, ordinances, and attachments are indexed through the City's official Legistar portal (`fairfaxva.legistar.com`). However, the official codified City Code, including Chapter 110 (Zoning), is hosted and served exclusively by **Municode**, a commercial aggregator on the hard blocklist.

Under Swapp Technologies LLC record integrity policy (**PROVENANCE.md**):
> "Sourcing: Primary government portals only. Hard blocklist: Municode, AmLegal (amlegal.com), eCode360, EnCodePlus."  
> "A jurisdiction that is empty because its code lives only on blocklisted aggregators is a **valid, recorded outcome** (`SOURCING_BLOCKED.md` noting what was checked) — never a gap to paper over."

Ingesting zoning regulations from Municode is prohibited. Automated polling and attachment extraction of individual signed ordinances from the Legistar calendar is scheduled for Phase 2. The City of Fairfax remains documented and unpopulated in accordance with the release rules.
