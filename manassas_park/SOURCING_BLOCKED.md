## 2. Evaluation & Sourcing Determination
The City of Manassas Park enacts zoning ordinances through its Governing Body. The Governing Body's legislative calendars, agenda packets, and meeting records are indexed through the City's official Legistar portal (`manassaspark.legistar.com`). However, the codified Code of the City of Manassas Park, including Chapter 31 (Zoning), is published and hosted solely on **Municode**, a commercial aggregator on the hard blocklist.

Under Swapp Technologies LLC record integrity policy (**PROVENANCE.md**):
> "Sourcing: Primary government portals only. Hard blocklist: Municode, AmLegal (amlegal.com), eCode360, EnCodePlus."  
> "A jurisdiction that is empty because its code lives only on blocklisted aggregators is a **valid, recorded outcome** (`SOURCING_BLOCKED.md` noting what was checked) — never a gap to paper over."

Ingesting zoning regulations from Municode is prohibited. Automated extraction of individual adopted ordinances from the Legistar calendar is scheduled for Phase 2. The City of Manassas Park remains documented and unpopulated in compliance with the release rules.
